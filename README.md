Solmar — Streets of Sun: an original open-world crime game in the browser. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Solmar police as your wanted level rises, and run nine story missions for fixer Lena Cruz as Marco Solano — from stealing your first wheels to the Palms Heist, the Tower Run, the four-star Getaway, a street race against a rival driver, and the five-star Last Job. Between jobs, drive taxi fares across the city for cash (double after dark), race for prize money from the start line by the stadium, hunt twelve hidden sun packages, and spend it all at the garage on repairs or a gold sport car. The day turns to night over four minutes: windows light up, streetlights glow, headlights cut the dark. Top-down 2D canvas, traffic and buses, pedestrians and beach joggers, combat, a HUD with minimap, save, and WebAudio-synthesised sound. No libraries, no network, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
