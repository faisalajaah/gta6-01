Solmar — Streets of Sun: an original open-world crime game in the browser. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Solmar police as your wanted level rises, and run six story missions for fixer Lena Cruz as Marco Solano — from stealing your first wheels to the Palms Heist and the Tower Run. Between jobs, drive taxi fares across the city for cash and spend it at the garage on repairs or a gold sport car. Top-down 2D canvas, traffic and pedestrians, combat, a HUD with minimap, save, and WebAudio-synthesised sound. No libraries, no network, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
